Application Requirements

Prepare the following documents for a smooth application process. Admissions staff can assist you if you need clarification.

  • Original and photocopy of high school report card
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Birth certificate (PSA copy)
  • Two recent 2×2 ID photos
  • Completed application form

Admission Procedure

1. Submit documentsAdmissions Office
2. Take the entrance assessmentAs scheduled
3. Attend interview/orientationBy appointment
4. Confirm enrollmentRegistrar's Office

Our admissions team is ready to answer questions about programs, requirements and enrollment schedules.
