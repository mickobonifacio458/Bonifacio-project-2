College Programs

Our college programs combine strong academics with Catholic values to prepare graduates for professional life.

College of Management, Accountancy and Technology

Business, Technology & Accountancy

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration

The BSBA program prepares students for careers in business and management. Students develop competencies in financial analysis, marketing strategy, organizational management, and entrepreneurship.

Majors / Specializations

Financial Management
Marketing Management

Career Opportunities

Financial Analyst · Marketing Manager · HR Specialist · Entrepreneur · Business Consultant

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

The BSIT program develops future-ready IT professionals with skills in software development, networking, database management, and emerging technologies.

Career Opportunities

Software Developer · Network Administrator · Database Administrator · IT Consultant · Web Developer

Bachelor of Science in Accountancy

The BSA program develops competent and ethical accountants prepared for the CPA licensure examination, with expertise in accounting, auditing, taxation, and advisory services.

Career Opportunities

Certified Public Accountant · External Auditor · Tax Consultant · Financial Controller · Government Auditor

Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information System

The BSAIS program integrates accounting knowledge with information technology skills for designing, implementing, and managing modern accounting information systems.

Career Opportunities

IT Auditor · Systems Analyst · ERP Consultant · Database Administrator · Financial Systems Manager

Bachelor of Science in Management Accounting

The BSMA program focuses on strategic financial management and decision-making through cost analysis, budgeting, performance evaluation, and strategic planning.

Career Opportunities

Management Accountant · Cost Analyst · Budget Manager · Financial Planner · Business Consultant

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management

The BSTM program prepares students for the tourism and hospitality industry, including tourism planning, event management, and sustainable tourism development.

Career Opportunities

Tour Operations Manager · Hotel Manager · Travel Consultant · Event Coordinator · Tourism Officer

College of Education, Arts and Sciences

Education, Arts & Sciences

Bachelor of Elementary Education

The BEEd program prepares dedicated and competent educators for elementary education with strong pedagogical foundations and subject knowledge.

Major / Specialization

General Education

Career Opportunities

Elementary School Teacher · Education Specialist · Curriculum Developer · School Administrator

Bachelor of Secondary Education

The BSEd program trains future secondary school teachers with advanced subject knowledge and effective teaching methodologies.

Majors / Specializations

English · Filipino · Values Education · Science · Mathematics

Career Opportunities

High School Teacher · Subject Coordinator · Education Researcher · Guidance Counselor

Bachelor of Early Childhood Education

The BECEd program prepares educators specializing in early childhood development and education through meaningful learning experiences for young children.

Career Opportunities

Preschool Teacher · Early Childhood Specialist · Child Development Worker · Daycare Center Manager

Bachelor of Physical Education

The BPEd program develops competent physical education teachers and sports professionals through exercise science, sports coaching, and fitness instruction.

Career Opportunities

PE Teacher · Sports Coach · Fitness Instructor · Athletic Director · Sports Development Officer

Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy

The AB Philosophy program develops critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and ethical reflection through the study of existence, knowledge, values, and the human condition.

Career Opportunities

Philosophy Professor · Seminary Studies · Ethics Consultant · Writer/Journalist · Law School Preparation

Bachelor of Science in Psychology

The BS Psychology program provides a comprehensive understanding of human behavior, mental processes, and psychological well-being through counseling, assessment, and research.

Career Opportunities

Psychologist · Guidance Counselor · Human Resource Officer · Behavioral Therapist · Research Analyst
